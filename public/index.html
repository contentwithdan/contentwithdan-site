FAQ

Do I get usage rights and raw footage?

Every video comes with 30 days of paid usage rights as standard. Extra usage can be agreed upfront, or extended later on your best-performing pieces. Raw footage is available on request and is priced separately, depending on the deliverables, so just ask and we'll agree it.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included with every package. A full script and storyboard are approved before filming, which keeps things moving efficiently and keeps revisions to a minimum.

Can you travel or shoot on location?

Yes, and I'm always keen to bring my energy to on-location shoots! I'm UK-based and happy to travel, including for content abroad. Travel costs are agreed upfront and covered by the brand.
